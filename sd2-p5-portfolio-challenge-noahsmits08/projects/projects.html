Beer Website

On this website, you will find all kinds of specialty beers that you can buy in the Netherlands.

For each beer, you can see exactly what you want to know: the name, the brewery, its origin, the alcohol percentage, the taste, the ingredients, and much more.

You can search for a specific beer using the search bar, and the beers are organized into categories. There are also seasonal beers, such as beers that fit Oktoberfest or Christmas. Create an account to save your favorite beers and leave a review for beers you have tried.

The website works in both Dutch and English, and can be used on your phone, tablet, or computer. The website features a database.

Car Website

I made a car website where you can find a lot of different car brands from all over the world.

I’ve put all the specific details and full specs under every single car model, so you can check everything easily.

Above every list, you can see the brand name, which makes it super easy to navigate through the site and find what you are looking for.

Also, we have a really useful compare tool where you can put cars next to each other and compare them directly, so you can see which car actually has the best price and specs in general.

KFC Tycoon

We made a Fast food tycoon based on KFC, you start off by claiming a plot and you begin with a conveyor system with droppers that drop different types of chicken generating money.

You use the earnings to upgrade your restaurant so you’ll eventually have a whole working restaurant.
